Job Title: Machine Learning Engineer (NLP)
Company: Cobaltine AI Labs
Location: Boston, MA (hybrid)
Employment Type: Full-time

About the Role
Cobaltine AI Labs develops language-understanding tools for legal and compliance teams. We are looking for an ML/NLP Engineer to help build and ship models that search, classify and summarize long documents. You will move work from research notebooks into reliable production systems.

Responsibilities
- Train and fine-tune transformer models in PyTorch for classification and extraction tasks.
- Build embedding-based retrieval and semantic search pipelines, including vector indexing.
- Design evaluation sets and metrics to measure model quality and track regressions.
- Work with data engineers to prepare clean training data from large text corpora.
- Package models as services and monitor their accuracy and latency in production.
- Share findings with product and engineering teams through clear write-ups.

Required Skills
- Strong Python and hands-on experience with PyTorch.
- Practical NLP experience, such as text classification, named entity recognition or retrieval.
- Experience creating and using embeddings for similarity search.
- Good grounding in machine learning fundamentals and evaluation methods.
- Proficiency with SQL and data manipulation libraries such as pandas.

Preferred Skills
- Experience with Hugging Face Transformers and scikit-learn baselines.
- Familiarity with vector databases or FAISS.
- Experience serving models through an API such as FastAPI.
- Knowledge of MLOps tools such as MLflow.
- A graduate degree in a quantitative field.

Experience Level
Mid to senior. We are looking for 3 or more years of applied machine learning experience, with at least one NLP project shipped to production.

What We Offer
Research time, a conference budget, flexible hours and a collaborative team. We review applications on a rolling basis.
