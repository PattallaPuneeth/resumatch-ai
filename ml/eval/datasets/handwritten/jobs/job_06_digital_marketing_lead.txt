Job Title: Digital Marketing Lead
Company: Saffron & Slate Home Goods
Location: Manchester, UK (hybrid)
Employment Type: Full-time

About the Role
Saffron & Slate Home Goods is an online retailer of homeware and furniture. We are looking for a Digital Marketing Lead to own our search and paid acquisition strategy and to lead a small, ambitious team. You will be accountable for traffic, conversion and return on ad spend across our channels.

Responsibilities
- Plan and run SEO strategy, including technical audits, keyword research and content planning.
- Manage paid advertising across Google Ads, Google Shopping and social platforms.
- Set budgets, forecast results and report performance to senior leadership.
- Use analytics tools to track funnels, attribution and campaign outcomes.
- Lead and coach a team of three marketers and coordinate with freelancers and agencies.
- Run A/B tests on landing pages and ad creative to improve conversion.

Required Skills
- Proven experience in SEO, with measurable growth in organic traffic.
- Hands-on experience managing Google Ads campaigns and large budgets.
- Strong analytics skills with Google Analytics 4 or similar tools.
- Experience planning and delivering multi-channel campaigns.
- Clear written and verbal communication.

Preferred Skills
- Experience with Semrush, Ahrefs or similar research tools.
- Familiarity with email marketing platforms such as HubSpot.
- Experience building dashboards in Looker Studio.
- Previous people-management experience.

Experience Level
Senior. We are looking for 5 or more years in digital marketing, including at least 1 year leading others.

What We Offer
Hybrid working, a clothing and homeware discount, a training budget and clear progression. We review applications on a rolling basis.
