Job Title: Senior Backend Engineer
Company: Halcyon Ledger Technologies
Location: Chicago, IL (hybrid, three days on-site)
Employment Type: Full-time

About the Role
Halcyon Ledger Technologies builds billing and reconciliation software for mid-sized wholesalers. We are looking for a Senior Backend Engineer to own the design and delivery of our core transaction services. You will join a small team that values clean APIs, careful data modeling and dependable releases.

Responsibilities
- Design, build and maintain Node.js and TypeScript services that process millions of ledger events every day.
- Model and optimize data in PostgreSQL, including schema design, indexing and query tuning.
- Deploy and operate services on Kubernetes, working with the platform team on Helm charts, autoscaling and rollout strategies.
- Review code, mentor mid-level engineers and raise the bar on testing practices.
- Break down ambiguous product requirements into well-scoped technical work.
- Join the on-call rotation and lead blameless post-incident reviews.

Required Skills
- Strong production experience with Node.js and TypeScript.
- Solid Postgres skills, including query plans, indexing and migrations.
- Hands-on experience with Docker and K8s in a production environment.
- Experience designing and versioning REST APIs.
- Automated testing habits, including unit and integration tests.

Preferred Skills
- GraphQL and Redis.
- Message queues such as RabbitMQ or Kafka.
- Observability tooling such as Prometheus and Grafana.
- CI/CD pipelines with GitHub Actions.
- Prior work on billing, payments or other financial systems.

Experience Level
Senior. We expect 5 or more years of professional backend development, including at least 2 years owning services in production.

What We Offer
A collaborative engineering culture, a learning budget, flexible hours and generous parental leave. We review applications on a rolling basis and welcome candidates from all backgrounds.
