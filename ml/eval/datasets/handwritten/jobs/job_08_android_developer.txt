Job Title: Android Developer
Company: Trailnote Fitness
Location: Denver, CO (remote-friendly)
Employment Type: Full-time

About the Role
Trailnote Fitness makes an outdoor activity and training app used by more than a million people. We are looking for an Android Developer to build new features and keep our app fast, stable and well loved. You will work with designers, backend engineers and a small QA team.

Responsibilities
- Build and maintain Android features in Kotlin using Jetpack libraries and Jetpack Compose.
- Follow MVVM architecture and keep code modular and testable.
- Integrate REST APIs and handle offline behavior and data caching.
- Improve app startup time, battery use and crash-free session rates.
- Write unit and UI tests and take part in code review.
- Support releases through the Google Play Console.

Required Skills
- At least 2 years of professional Android development.
- Strong Kotlin, including coroutines and Flow.
- Experience with the Android SDK, Jetpack components and MVVM.
- Experience with Retrofit, Room or similar libraries.
- Familiarity with Git and CI workflows.

Preferred Skills
- Firebase services such as Authentication, Cloud Messaging and Crashlytics.
- Jetpack Compose in production apps.
- Experience with Java on Android is a plus.
- Espresso or other UI testing tools.

Experience Level
Mid-level. We are looking for 2 to 5 years of Android development experience. Strong candidates with published apps are encouraged to apply.

What We Offer
Remote-friendly working, an annual gear allowance, health benefits and a supportive team. We review applications on a rolling basis.
