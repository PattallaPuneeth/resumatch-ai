Job Title: DevOps Engineer
Company: Redfield Streaming Co.
Location: Atlanta, GA (hybrid)
Employment Type: Full-time

About the Role
Redfield Streaming Co. delivers live and on-demand video to millions of viewers. Our platform team is hiring a DevOps Engineer to keep our cloud infrastructure reliable, observable and easy for developers to use. You will build the tooling that lets product teams deploy safely many times a day.

Responsibilities
- Operate and improve our Kubernetes clusters, including upgrades, scaling and security hardening.
- Define cloud infrastructure with Terraform and review infrastructure changes with other engineers.
- Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines that test, scan and deploy services automatically.
- Set up monitoring, dashboards and alerting with Prometheus and Grafana.
- Respond to incidents, run post-incident reviews and drive follow-up improvements.
- Help developers troubleshoot deployment and performance problems.

Required Skills
- Hands-on production experience with Kubernetes and Docker.
- Strong working knowledge of Terraform or a similar infrastructure-as-code tool.
- Experience building CI/CD pipelines with tools such as GitLab CI, GitHub Actions or Jenkins.
- Experience with monitoring and alerting, such as Prometheus and Grafana.
- Scripting skills in Python or Bash.
- Experience with a major cloud provider, preferably AWS.

Preferred Skills
- Experience with Helm and GitOps tools such as ArgoCD.
- Knowledge of log aggregation using the ELK stack.
- Cost optimization experience.
- Experience managing databases in the cloud.

Experience Level
Mid to senior. We are looking for 4 or more years in DevOps, site reliability or platform engineering roles.

What We Offer
Competitive pay, on-call compensation, a learning budget and a collaborative team culture. We review applications on a rolling basis.
